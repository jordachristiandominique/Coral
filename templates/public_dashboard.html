Reading the survey

A guide to Hard Coral Cover

What is counted, how results are combined, and what the category tells you.

Start with one quadrat

Each image has ten representative markers allocated from reviewed coral boxes. Markers represent Hard Coral, Soft Coral, or Other Substrate.

From image to survey site

Image HCC values are averaged within each transect. The site result is the mean of those transect averages, so each transect has equal weight.

HCC describes hard coral cover. It does not measure coral health, bleaching, water quality, or biodiversity.
