What do these numbers mean?

Hard Coral Cover (HCC) is measured with the CPCE point-intercept method: sample points are placed on each survey image and classified, then Hard Coral Cover = Hard Coral points ÷ Total points surveyed × 100. Only scleractinian (stony) hard coral counts toward HCC. Categories are benchmarked against the Philippine national average of 22.8% and the Tubbataha Reefs benchmark.

Category A (more than 44%) — reef coral cover is more than double the national average, comparable to or exceeding Tubbataha Reefs. Category B (more than 33% up to 44%) — coral cover exceeds both the national average and the Tubbataha benchmark. Category C (more than 22% up to 33%) — coral cover is above the national average, though below the Tubbataha benchmark. Category D (0-22%) — coral cover is at or below the national average; this reef may still provide meaningful ecosystem services and habitat.
